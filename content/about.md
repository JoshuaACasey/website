Joshua is an autistic human, nerdy video game streamer and digital rights activist that is passionate about human rights, free speech, anti-censorship, privacy, autistic people’s rights, sex worker’s rights, and generally just making the world a better place for those of us that aren't billionaires. From December 2021 to September 2025, he volunteered to do social media for [Prostasia Foundation](https://prostasia.org/about/), a prevention of child sexual abuse organization that is focused on preventing sexual abuse WITHOUT violating the human rights and free speech that we usually take for granted. As of March 2025, he volunteers to do social media for the anti-censorship and human rights organization [Center for Online Safety and Liberty](https://c4osl.org).
